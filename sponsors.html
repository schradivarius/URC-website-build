Partner with us

Put your name on a Mars rover

We are a student team with a professional-grade problem: build a planetary rover on a student budget and take it to Utah. Our sponsors make that arithmetic work — and get first look at the engineers who pulled it off.

Why it matters

What your support buys

01

A hiring pipeline

Our members graduate having designed, fabricated, tested and field-repaired real hardware under deadline. Sponsors get resume books, lab visits and first contact with them.

02

Visibility

Your logo travels on the rover, on the team kit and on the video that goes to the judges — alongside our outreach across Houston schools and the wider aerospace community.

03

Direct impact

Every dollar goes into parts, materials, machining and travel. Nothing here is theoretical: sponsorship is the difference between a CAD model and a rover in the desert.

Not everything is cash

Some of the most valuable support we get never touches our budget line.

  • Raw stock: aluminium, carbon fibre, fasteners.
  • Machining, waterjet, anodising and PCB fabrication.
  • Motors, drives, batteries, radios, sensors and compute.
  • CAD, simulation and EDA software licences.
  • Engineering mentorship and design review time.
