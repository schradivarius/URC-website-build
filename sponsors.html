The Houston Apollos design and build a planetary rover on a student budget and take it to competition in Utah. Our sponsors make that possible, and gain early access to the engineers who build it.

Our Sponsors

The organizations supporting the Houston Apollos this season.

Founding sponsor

Kirby Inland Marine

Kirby Inland Marine supports the Houston Apollos' first season, funding the materials, components and travel required to bring a rover to competition. We are grateful for their early commitment to the team.

Why Sponsor the Apollos

Access to Talent

Our members graduate having designed, fabricated, tested and field-repaired real hardware under deadline. Sponsors get resume books, lab visits and first contact with them.

Brand Visibility

Your logo travels on the rover, on the team kit and in the video that goes to the judges — alongside our outreach across Houston schools and the wider aerospace community.

Direct Impact

URC caps what a team may spend on fielded equipment at $26,000, so the rules set the ceiling on our rover and fundraising sets the floor. Every dollar goes into parts, materials, machining and travel.

Sponsorship Levels

Support may be given in cash or in kind. Materials, machining time, components and software licenses are recognized at fair market value, and we are glad to assemble a package suited to your organization.

In-Kind Support

Some of the most valuable support the team receives never appears on a budget line.

  • Raw stock: aluminum, carbon fiber, fasteners.
  • Machining, waterjet, anodising and PCB fabrication.
  • Motors, drives, batteries, radios, sensors and compute.
  • CAD, simulation and EDA software licenses.
  • Engineering mentorship and design review time.
