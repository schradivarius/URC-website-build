Recruiting now

You don't need experience. You need to show up.

Most of our members joined knowing nothing about rovers. What they had was curiosity and a few hours a week. We will teach you the rest — CAD, machining, firmware, ROS, field operations, whatever your subteam runs on.

Why join

Real hardware, real deadlines, real consequences

Coursework gives you problems with answers in the back of the book. This gives you a rover that either climbs the hill in Utah or doesn't.

  • Own a real subsystem, from requirements through field testing.
  • Learn the tools industry actually uses — and put them on your resume honestly.
  • Work alongside students from every college on campus.
  • Travel with the team to competition once you have earned your spot.
  • Teach the next cohort what you learned. That is how this team survives.

Process

How to join

Come to a general meeting

No application, no interview, no obligation. Show up and meet the team.

Pick a subteam

Sit in on a couple of subteam meetings and choose where you want to spend your semester.

Take a starter task

Your lead hands you something small and real. You will be paired with a returning member while you learn the tools.

Own something

Deliver on the small things and the big ones follow — a subsystem, a design review, a seat in the operations crew at competition.

Questions

Before you ask

Do I need to be an engineering major?

No. Engineering and computer science make up most of the team, but we need geologists for the science mission, business and communications students for sponsorship and outreach, and designers for everything we publish.

I am a freshman with zero experience. Is that a problem?

It is the opposite of a problem — freshmen have the most time to grow into a lead role. We train everyone from scratch, and the fastest way to learn is on a team where the hardware has to work.

How much time does it take?

Plan on 5–10 hours a week, concentrated around design reviews and integration. Leads put in more. Be honest with your lead about your course load and we will scope your work to match.

Can I join mid-semester?

Yes. Recruiting is heaviest at the start of each semester, but there is always work to pick up. Send us a message and we will point you at the right subteam lead.
